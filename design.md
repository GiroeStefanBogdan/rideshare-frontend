# Design System Specification: Heritage in Motion (DrumBun)

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
**The Creative North Star: "The Digital Weaver"**

This design system for **DrumBun** rejects the cold, transactional nature of commercial ride-sharing. Instead, it draws inspiration from the Romanian concept of *comunitate* (community) and the tactile beauty of traditional folk art. The aesthetic is **Modern Folk Minimalist**: clean, high-utility digital interfaces grounded by the timeless geometry of Romanian wood-carving and 'IA' (traditional blouse) embroidery.

## 2. Visual Identity & Brand Voice
*   **Tone:** Trustworthy, neighborly, cultural, and professional.
*   **Personality:** Like a helpful neighbor who knows the old stories but uses a smartphone.
*   **Mission:** To facilitate community-driven transport through a non-profit, collaborative vibe.

## 3. Color Palette: The Voroneț Foundation
*   **Voroneț Blue (#003399):** The primary brand color. Used for key actions (CTAs), headers, and prominent UI accents. Evokes trust and Romanian heritage.
*   **Soft White (#F8F9FA):** The primary background color. Provides a clean, airy canvas that allows the folk motifs to breathe.
*   **Earthy Charcoal (#333333):** Used for primary typography and secondary UI elements to maintain high readability and professional contrast.
*   **Heritage Gray (#F3F4F5):** Used for subtle backgrounds, dividers, and decorative motifs.

## 4. Typography: Modern Clarity
*   **Primary Font:** **Manrope** (or similar modern Sans-Serif).
*   **Scale:**
    *   **H1:** Bold, tracking-tight. Used for emotional hero headlines.
    *   **H2/H3:** Semi-bold. Used for section headers.
    *   **Body:** Regular, leading-relaxed for optimal legibility.
    *   **Accents:** All-caps with increased letter-spacing for labels (e.g., "PLECĂM DIN...").

## 5. The Folk Motifs (The Heart of the System)
*   **The Geometric Pattern:** A repeating motif based on traditional wood-carving (rhombuses, X-shapes, and sun-circles). Used as low-opacity background layers in hero sections.
*   **The 'IA' Border:** Subtle, 1-2px geometric borders applied to specific modules like the Search Card or Ride Cards.
*   **Section Dividers:** Instead of simple lines, use a repeating "saw-tooth" or geometric pattern at low opacity to separate major content areas.

## 6. Component Guidelines
*   **Search Module:** A floating, white card with a subtle shadow. Inputs are labeled clearly in a smaller, all-caps font. The primary 'Căută Curse' button uses the Voroneț Blue.
*   **Ride Cards:**
    *   Emphasize the human element: Large driver photos with circular masks.
    *   Transparency: Clear 'Cost Share' pricing with no hidden fees.
    *   Trust: Prominent star ratings and review counts.
*   **Buttons:** Softly rounded corners (8px) with smooth hover transitions.

## 7. Interaction & Motion
*   **Transitions:** Gentle fades and slight vertical shifts (10-20px) when elements appear.
*   **Hover States:** Subtle scaling or opacity changes on interactive cards to reinforce the "neighborly" feel.
